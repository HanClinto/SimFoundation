## The Selected Showcase

This is the one selected containment showcase; SCP-173 is not implemented
alongside it. The source emphasizes acting preparation and regularly showing
a new, extremely low-quality horror/science-fiction work. Its poor recognition
of human fear is the premise for rehearsed hosting, not permission to build an
unbounded psychological model.

The adaptation has a fixed audience figure, a physical rehearsal station,
three original approved prints and an unreviewed print. A hosting session means
a trained worker performing the bounded routine for the actual nearby
audience over elapsed work. It does not measure real fear or simulate arbitrary
shape changes, continuous surveillance, surprise dialogue or a catastrophic
breach. The tick cadence is not a conversion of the article's monthly schedule.

## Physical Rehearsal And Programmes

`brief screening` introduces the retained annex. Each host must
`order ben study rehearsal acting-rehearsal` beside the actual guide. The
dated finding belongs to that actor; training Ben does not qualify Alex.
Repeated rehearsal does not duplicate the same actor's finding.

Carry/deliver an approved print beside `rig` at (8,5), then
`order ben service rig` or `assign ben rig`. The actual SCP-2006 audience must
remain within two tiles. Untrained staff, missing audience, unreviewed material,
previously completed prints and damaged equipment produce explicit blockers.

The prints are original game fiction:

- **The Terrible Teapot**
- **The Cardboard Moon**
- **Fog from the Cupboard**

They are ordinary, non-stackable physical objects. Presenting a print retains
its identity and amount. Its completed receipt records `consumed: false`, and
the same print cannot qualify as a new programme again at that rig. Moving it
away and back does not reset that history. Unreviewed footage is not accepted
or played; no arbitrary content is loaded.

An active presentation claims its reusable print through the host's queue.
Another worker cannot pick it up until the presentation completes or is
cancelled. Cancellation leaves the print available and creates no completed
screening. A disappeared/damaged active print cannot create a phantom result.
Current-version replay preserves the same commitment and history.

## Continuing Work

A complete ten-tick session covers 160 ticks, with the next programme becoming
due 32 ticks early. The host uses the same Service/duty and critical food/rest
selection as the diner. Stage future unused prints near the rig before leaving
the host at the annex. There are only three prints and two initial meals;
programme exhaustion and staff hunger are real limits, not automatic resupply.

Lapsed coverage blocks rig activities until another unused approved print is
shown. The supplied programme list is finite; a new catalogue print would need
an explicit future content decision, not a cloning command. No lethal effect
or source-wide shapeshifting escalation is invented on lapse.

The [normal-command walkthrough](tests/curated-hosting.txt) trains two staff,
stages three prints, leaves an actual host in the annex and observes depletion:

```sh
npm run sim < src/simulation/catalog/quests/scp2006/tests/curated-hosting.txt
```

Core version 19 adds actor-specific study scope and reusable/distinct service
input contracts. Earlier development saves are discarded without migration.
