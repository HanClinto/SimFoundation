## Game Adaptation

Addendum131-1 describes the pair watching SCP-173 and backing out with the
human cleaning crew. The human procedure continues unchanged. General warden
applications are only being considered, not established or certified.

The original supervised local-visit scenario adds specifically authored
supplemental gaze on SCP-173 only, requiring current sight and a nearby visible
conscious human. It does not replace the formal human crew. The six-tile
proximity rule, cooperative visit staging, small nonblocking map footprint and
ordinary travel timing are game abstractions, not source measurements.

No food or sleep upkeep is invented. Full affection/bond dynamics, independent
roaming, braking accidents, climbing, hourly audit/lockdown administration,
injury simulation and generalized warden
deployment are outside this cut. No claim of invulnerability, obedience or
general-purpose camera behavior follows from these omissions.
