Abby’s SCP Recommendations for Abigail and Chase

So, after you’re done reading these you can explore on your own around the website, obviously. I recommend starting with the “Most Popular List”, linked here: http://www.scp-wiki.net/top-rated-pages . But here are some of my personal favorites to get you started! Also, note, SCPs are rated in three categories (there’s a fourth but it’s so rarely used I don’t really count it): Safe (easily contained but far from harmless), Euclid (requires special containment but is relatively easily contained so long as those conditions are met), and Keter (difficult to contain properly and require immense amounts of care/upkeep to keep them securely locked away).

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-001-o5 - The Factory.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-049 - The Plague Doctor. One of my favorites for the sheer aesthetic and creep factors. 

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-073 and http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-076 - A familiar old story retold

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-087 - What could be so scary about a staircase?

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-093 - Alternate realities are scary. This one is long and goes deep.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-096 - Once you see its face, there’s no going back.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-106 - Oh, you know. Just an old man who drags you into endless torture dimensions. No big deal. Here’s a connected story. Don’t read before eating: http://www.scp-wiki.net/treats And another one that discusses his possible origin: http://www.scp-wiki.net/the-young-man

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-173 - This is the most famous SCP, arguably the one that is iconic with the site. It’s not my favorite, personally, but it is a suitable introduction for what you’re about to get in to.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-179 - A lookout for all of us from hazards in space

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-191 - A little girl turned into a computer

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-231 - Seriously one of the most fascinatingly messed up things I’ve ever read, made all the more terrifying because of what the authors chooses not to tell you. Just a perfect example that things are so much scarier/horrifying when we’re allowed to supplement our own interpretations. 

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-294 - The vending machine that puts all other vending machines to shame.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-342 - The only train ticket you will ever need, the last one you’ll ever use.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-348 - Here’s a super sweet one about a bowl of soup.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-439 - I’m not going to sleep ever again.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-504 - Tomatoes who do not approve of your jokes.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-507 - Teleportation no longer seems as fun as it once did.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-529 - Half a cat, all the loves.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-610 - The Flesh. Took me a few readings to get all the way through it.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-701 - You like theater stuff, right? Yeah, not after this.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-745 - The Headlights. Do not read if you plan to go driving in the dark anytime soon.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-835 - Horrifying, sad, and well written. Be sure to read the “clean” version after the redacted one-- it’s a good example of how powerful things are when they both are and are not shown.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-953 - This one gets graphic, but it’s an interesting take on what happens when humans forget how to interact with the myths they create

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-993 - I always knew PBS was up to something.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-999 - One of the few SCPs who are genuinely invested in making human’s lives better. It’s also a sentient orange blob.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1000 - The only thing worse than humanity is ourselves.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1048 - It’s just a teddy bear. I swear.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1171 - A funnier one after the one above probably traumatized you a bit. Racist aliens? Yes please.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1281 - A voice from beyond our galaxy with an important message

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1437 - It’s a hole in the ground.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1762 - What happens when we forget what it means to believe in Fantasy?

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-1867 - Don’t bother trying to tell him he’s a sea slug, he won’t believe you.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-3008 - A Hotel California IKEA who’s staff are just so polite. So, so polite.

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-3333 - Humanity’s curiosity will be its own demise, as per usual

http://www.scp-wiki.net/scp-3393 - How do you stop a self-aware meme?
