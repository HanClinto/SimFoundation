# Physical Containment And Emergency Lockdown

The original kinetic subject now has a complete home intake and failure loop.
A compatible cell must be repaired and provisioned by ordinary Service before
Contain can carry the restrained subject to it and spend intake work. The cell
owns the same subject through the existing carried-parent relationship; there
is no parallel prisoner inventory or quest-owned lifecycle.

Coverage derives from completed service receipts. Safe containment suppresses
independent movement and pauses transport-restraint struggle. Unrestrain can
then physically recover the original band, enabling reuse rather than an
indefinitely attached hidden restraint. The cell's physical Study can inspect
its own contained living subject; a dead body cannot satisfy a living-source
study by retaining the same definition ID.

Forty ticks before service expiry the contained subject's custody turn emits
a warning. Expired primary coverage releases it at the authored physical
hatch, with a breach event and all injuries, charges and identity retained.
A worker can instead perform emergency Lockdown with a real maintenance pack
and three work ticks. That buys eighty ticks of containment; it does not
restore power supplies or permanently postpone service. Ten ticks before
lockdown expiry there is another warning. Cancelled funded work retains its
spent input, and existing facility occupancy prevents concurrent control work.

The normal-command containment cycle provisions the cell, equips a worker,
subdues and restrains the original hostile, transfers it home, admits and
studies it, recovers the band, invokes fallback, permits a lapse, and spends
the remaining intervention charge to re-restrain/recontain. The same finite
reserve system can recover from lost workers, not magically restore them.

Core version 24 adds cell/fallback state and discards incompatible development
saves. This is a small explicit original scenario, not a general containment
language or a claim that peaceful SCP display/research fixtures implement
these dangerous semantics.

Focused review caught three lifecycle gaps before publication. Intake now
emits a warning when it completes after the initial empty-cell warning point.
The intake study explicitly requires sources owned by effective containment
throughout productive work, not merely nearby living cargo. Deliver now invokes
ordinary Take for recoverable body-owned items, not just ground items, so the
expanded recovery command cannot walk away without collecting the property.
Each fix has a normal-command regression.
