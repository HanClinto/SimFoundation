# Reusable campaign transport

Player feedback in #40 supersedes the initial finite-trip experiment.
Transport should enable research, intervention and staff relief, not impose
seven lifetime journeys regardless of preparation.

Remove transport dockets and their readiness/payment/reward rules rather than
retaining an unlimited-ticket facade. Ordinary routes remain repeatable through
the same physical departure/transfer machinery. No vehicle, fare or fuel economy
is introduced. Duration, manifests, loading capacity, research gates, outbound
staff readiness, operating windows, blocked admission and transit health/custody
all remain authoritative.

Kestrel's former three tickets become three finite maintenance packs: useful
physical recovery, not another entitlement ledger. Reserve dispatch needs no
parallel token budget because its two actual people already bound recruitment.
Once either leaves the reserve, that same person joins the ordinary roster and
travels normally; no replacement is instantiated.

Existing consumable scarcity remains. More journeys cost time while all sites
advance, and cannot regenerate food, medicine, equipment or casualties. A future
fleet-capacity economy must justify itself separately. Development saves using
the removed definitions are discarded through the normal version boundary.
