# Restraint Is Not Consent

The original hostile subject requires an actual compatible restraint for live
transport even while temporarily subdued. Restrain physically approaches the
subject, requires incapacity, and spends work attaching the carried item to
the subject. The item remains its own entity in the same carried ownership
tree as the subject and all equipment. There is no case-packing of living
people and no change to `acceptsEscort`.

A recovering subject under an effective restraint cannot choose independent
work, but can be physically carried or follow an explicit supervised escort.
Its restraint loses condition on each conscious tick, including transit.
Breakage leaves the broken item on the ground, releases carried custody
physically and emits an identified escape event. A loose hostile in transit
still belongs to the transfer until arrival; it is not deleted or teleported
into a second site. Admission may now introduce a dangerous loose subject,
which is why restraint condition is visible before travel.

Dead/subdued subjects do not consume struggle wear. Take can recover an
attached restraint from a body and clears its attached flag. Active restraints
are not loose hand cargo or wearable armor. Generic transfer requires effective
restraints for entities explicitly marked as requiring them, not for every
ordinary staff member with a hostile-faction policy.

Custody runs once after physiology locally or in transit. Transfer clones
mutable carried items before advancing struggle so input snapshots are not
mutated. Save version 23 discards older development shapes. Restraint alone
is temporary and not a containment system; physical containment, warning and
fallback work follows next.
