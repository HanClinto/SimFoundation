# Recorded research and bounded crafting

The progression direction in #40 needs an actual observation -> design ->
physical build -> useful capability loop, not abstract research-point rewards.

Keep the existing dated `Finding` as evidence. A shared, deterministic local
record query serves route gates, actor-specific training and recipe prerequisites.
The first engineering study requires the same living kinetic subject under
effective holding for all productive work; mere damage, death, collection or
an ordinary research-needs activity does not award this discovery.

One `Craft` action uses one authored supply definition/quantity and an explicit
item blueprint. It approaches a real bench, consumes accessible portions when
productive work begins, and creates one grounded output only after completion.
No arbitrary recipe expressions, multi-stage planner, global tech graph or
second executor are introduced. Catalog owns the kinetic study, recipe and band.
The one material requirement may span multiple stacks. Shared supply eligibility
and stable source-ID order select the full quantity before anything is spent;
an insufficient total or another worker's carried stack cannot partly fund it.

Funding remains in the ordinary queued action. This retains a bench lease even
after progress resets until actual cancellation/failure/interruption/completion.
Cancelled work loses the spent materials and creates no output. New commands
cannot carry paid markers forward. A per-bench output counter supplies stable
identities; the previous local output must be cleared before another build.

The output stores its maker, bench, dates, consumed sources/amounts and the actual
finding snapshot (including original observer and source IDs). It remains an
ordinary physical item through pickup, restraints, care, transport and breakage.
No separate crafting-inventory or output registry duplicates ownership.

The first recipe costs two maintenance packs for a kinetic-specific band with
half normal conscious wear: 400 rather than 200 awake ticks from fresh condition.
It competes with repair/fallback supplies and still eventually breaks. This
does not make the subject cooperative or generate medicine/suppression charges.
Disaster observation needs a real recording/witness mechanism in a later slice;
it is not inferred from an omniscient event history here.
