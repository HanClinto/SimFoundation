# Ordinary Roster Without Special Reserves

## Decision

The user rejected the dedicated reserve pair as hard-coded and chose:
"Only the ordinary starting roster; remove the special reserve mechanism."

Remove the regional reserve site, named-responder whitelist, dispatch function,
application/CLI command and browser controls. Alex, Ben and Casey remain the
ordinary authored starting roster. No recruiting, requesting replacements or
adding the former reserve people to the home roster is introduced.

This supersedes the reserve-response portion of decision029. Permanent death,
physical bodies/equipment, ordinary travel, casualty handling and care remain.
Surviving colleagues can perform recovery. If every starting staff member dies,
the world remains inspectable but no hidden personnel appear; the player can
start a new campaign. Incapacitated but living staff are not declared dead.

Remove the alternate-pad `arrivalMode` exception added solely for reserve
dispatch. Ordinary group landing still requires a clear primary pad and enough
bounded nearby space for the complete manifest; blocked transit retains real
people, health and cargo. Managers must keep routes and arrival areas usable.

## Consequences

Three-person SCP-173 work uses the three original staff, transferred through
ordinary trips. Walkthroughs and regression scenarios must use surviving staff,
not inject an extra campaign roster to preserve the removed recovery shortcut.
Isolated mechanics tests may still author their own appropriate fixtures.

Current campaign status and the browser response desk explicitly explain total
roster loss. Core version49 and session version7 discard development saves
containing the removed site/transfer exception; no migrations are added.

Staff acquisition can be designed later if requested, rather than being hidden
inside a reserve fallback.
