# Physical Equipment And Temporary Subdual

Intervention equipment is an actual item carried by its wearer, with a worn
flag and one explicit tool/armor slot. The pawn does not copy equipment stats,
charges or condition. Worn objects do not occupy the single loose-cargo slot;
normal ownership expansion includes them in transit and on a retained body.
Equip/unequip are physical pawn actions. Body-owned items can be recovered by
another worker; living ownership cannot be overridden by these orders.

The fictional suppression instrument enables Subdue only while worn and with
a charge. Work physically approaches a hostile living subject. One completed
intervention spends one charge and records temporary subdual expiry, distinct
from injury and consent. Expiry restores only supported subdual incapacity,
not unrelated wounds or death. This slice intentionally does not claim that
an unconscious hostile is restrained or contained.

Actual worn armor reduces modeled impact severity and loses condition on each
impact. Existing default combat capabilities remain; there is no wholesale
legacy equipment port or weapon crafting. The original kinetic specimen adds
bleeding impacts and a visible lethal-risk route. Sending actual staff there
opts their health into twelve-critical-tick mortality, retained after return.
Other existing routes do not silently change their patients' mortality.

The reserve recovery path remains finite. A dead wearer retains gear; a
replacement responder can physically equip that same depleted gear rather than
receive a free new loadout. The intervention transcript demonstrates subdual
and withdrawal; explicit restraints and containment are the next dependency.
Core version 22 discards older development saves without migrations.
