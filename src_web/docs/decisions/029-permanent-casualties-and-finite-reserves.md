# Permanent Casualties And Finite Reserves

The reserve-response portion is superseded by
[decision043](043-ordinary-roster-without-special-reserves.md). The dedicated
pair and dispatch mechanism were removed; permanent-casualty behavior remains.
The rest of this record describes the historical decision.

The continuation review makes death and subdued living recovery intended
capabilities, not permanent exclusions implied by earlier peaceful checkpoints.
This slice establishes irreversible loss and a finite response path first.

Mortality is explicit optional health data. Existing peaceful scenarios retain
their incapacitation behavior. An opted-in person accumulates consecutive
critical ticks while bleeding at maximum blood loss or suffering critical
wound/brain trauma. Stabilizing bleeding interrupts the first condition; it
does not erase injury or heal critical trauma. At the authored threshold,
death records cause and simulation tick. Site and transit physiology share the
same check, so blocked admission cannot suspend or double a critical interval.

The dead pawn remains the same physical body with its carried ownership tree.
It stops physiology, autonomy and all queued work, becomes nonblocking, and can
be carried home. Paid work is not refunded. Treatment, clinical recovery and
organ mending cannot restore life. There is no corpse conversion, silent
deletion or second personnel ledger.

The campaign has two actual reserve responders at a retained regional site
and two physical reserved dispatch allocations. `reserve <route|home> <name>`
transfers one unused responder through ordinary transit, then adds that
identity to the controllable roster. It creates neither a pawn nor a rescue
result. The responder must still travel to patients/bodies, carry them and
return; blocked admission remains physical. A wiped initial roster can thus
continue, but consuming and losing both reserves has real finite consequences.

The original accident demonstrates prompt stabilization or late body recovery
with a retained personal recorder. Critical/dead state is explicit in CLI maps
and campaign status. Core version 21 discards incompatible development saves;
no migrations or deep validators were added. Equipment-backed danger and
restraint/containment follow this prerequisite rather than being relabelled
cooperative escort.
