# Specific Patchwork Organ Care

## Decision

The approved SCP-2295 slice adds only lung and brain trauma facts, not a
universal anatomy/surgery model. Health distinguishes organ trauma from wounds
and accumulated blood loss. A replacement retains its own dated material
provenance on the organ; no duplicate patient or synthetic research inventory
is created.

Named SCP-2295 data supplies a narrow organ-mending capability. Core knows its
range, supported organ, work duration and material requirement, not the SCP
identity. Ordinary autonomy offers a Mend action for the youngest eligible
nearby human. The ordinary queue owns work and paid input. A changed youngest
patient or loss of proximity interrupts an already-funded attempt, retaining
the spent fabric and granting no partial replacement.

An external ground textile bundle is preferred. Otherwise a bounded amount of
the mender's own material is consumed through the shared material helper,
retaining a minimum body reserve. This does not implement automatic stuffing
regeneration or fabric repair. An unsupported youngest patient's injury blocks
replacement instead of being healed or ignored.

A completed lung replacement preserves other wounds and blood loss and sets
postoperative incapacity. The existing Nurse course now supports that state:
it needs a physical clinical pack and a completed course even when blood loss
is already zero. Only supported health-caused incapacity can clear; a brain
injury or severe unrelated wound still prevents recovery.

Core version 16 discards incompatible development snapshots. Tests exercise two
adult ages, distinct IDs, exact consumed source identity, self-material
depletion, interruption, a younger arrival, unsupported brain trauma, replay and
paid postoperative care. The scenario transcript is an actual two-patient
carry/return/treatment loop, never runtime-imported solution code.

## Limits

This is not a general organ transplantation API, a clinical simulator, a death
system or a shortcut around ordinary follow-up care. Source attribution and
the omitted mechanics live beside SCP-2295. Generic mechanisms remain in core;
patient ages, named content, physical fixtures and adaptation decisions remain
in catalog.
