# Direct watch and supervised work

After proving the separate SCP-2006 programme loop, #40's reverse-stealth
direction justifies a bounded direct-attention source rather than another
collection task.

Use one ordinary finite `Watch` commitment. Active coverage derives from
existing queues, human capability, control, fatigue and current sight. There is
no saved observer registry or second scheduler. It starts on the worker's turn;
handoffs must overlap actual active work. Duration/fatigue warnings give time
to arrange relief. Inactive, blocked, incapable, carried, restrained, nonhuman
or unauthorized observers never provide coverage.

Expiration happens on the observer's turn after its final committed interval,
not immediately after activation/increment. Even a one-tick watch therefore
holds one subject turn, regardless of which actor sorts first. Current sight,
fatigue and capability are still checked at every coverage query.

An opted-in pawn stays still while covered and resets attack windup rather than
banking an instant hit. Its physiology remains ordinary. Threat assessment
excludes a currently held source, so assigned supervised work and care can
actually proceed. Geometry lives in `Sight.ts`; threat/attention queries reuse
it without a circular dependency. Inspection does not offer impossible
autonomous behavior for a held source.

An optional facility supervision rule requires a nearby named subject and
distinct direct observers beside the station, excluding its worker. Service
and Study recheck this before productive work. One observer may physically
freeze the source while still being insufficient for a two-observer procedure.

The SCP-173 annex requires prior actual kinetic-intake research and three real
people. A glass gallery supports staging; the normal procedure moves observers
inside under overlapping coverage, admits the third worker and closes the door
before work. Withdrawal reverses this under coverage. Automatic blinking,
exact source speed, cameras and source transport are explicitly deferred.
The old SCP-2006 source remains a distinct authored capability, not a shared
psychological framework.
